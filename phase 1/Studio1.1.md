# The Last Transmission

## Poster Pitch

Create a science fiction movie poster set inside an abandoned space station on a distant planet. The main subject is a lone astronaut standing in front of a huge glowing futuristic doorway. Use sharp geometric lines in the walls and buildings to create a strong structured feeling, and use dramatic diagonal lines that lead the viewer’s eyes toward the astronaut and the glowing doorway. The main shapes should be large rectangular metal structures and a large circular doorway in the center of the composition. Use positive space around the astronaut and doorway, while leaving large areas of dark negative space around them to create a feeling of loneliness and isolation. The surfaces of the station should have rough, scratched, and damaged metallic textures, while the astronaut’s suit should have a smoother texture. The doorway should appear massive and heavy compared with the small human figure standing in front of it. Create strong value contrast between the bright blue-white light coming from the doorway and the deep shadows surrounding the astronaut. Make the glowing doorway and the astronaut the main focal point of the poster. The overall mood should be mysterious, dramatic, lonely, and dangerous.

## Production Review

In my first poster, the lines help guide my attention toward the center of the image. The tall vertical lines of the station make the space feel large and powerful, while the circular lines around the doorway bring my eyes toward the astronaut. The diagonal lines on the sides also help create movement toward the center. I think this makes the poster feel dramatic and isolated because the astronaut looks very small compared with the station.

The glowing doorway is the element that stands out the most. The bright blue-white light creates a strong contrast with the dark areas around it. The astronaut is also easy to notice because the figure is placed directly in front of the light. The large circular shape of the doorway and the smaller shape of the astronaut create a strong difference in scale and volume.

One thing the algorithm changed from my original idea was the amount of detail in the station. It added a lot of signs, writing, cables, and mechanical details that I did not specifically describe. However, these details helped make the station look like a real science fiction environment.

For my second attempt, I changed the texture from rough and damaged metal to smooth and polished metal. This single change made the station look newer and more advanced. The first version feels abandoned, dangerous, and old, while the second version feels cleaner, more controlled, and technologically advanced. The composition stayed almost the same, but the texture changed the overall mood significantly.
